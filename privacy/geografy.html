Geografy for iOS

Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Geografy collects nothing. There is no server, no analytics and no advertising. Your progress stays on your device, and the developer never receives any of it.

What the app stores

Geografy keeps the following on your device only:

  • Your learning progress: XP, streaks, quiz and game results, flashcard reviews, and achievements.
  • Countries you mark as visited or want to visit, favorites, and travel journal entries and photos.
  • Your settings, such as appearance, map style, and reminders.
  • If you sign in with Apple, the name and email Apple shares with the app, and a sign-in identifier kept in the device Keychain.

None of this is sent to the developer or shared with anyone else. Home Screen widgets read it from the same device.

Network requests

Geografy works offline. When a connection is available, it fetches public data that contains nothing about you:

  • Currency exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API.
  • Country statistics from the World Bank.
  • Images from Wikimedia Commons, and Wikipedia articles you choose to open.

Like any web request, these reveal your device's IP address to the service that answers it. Each service handles that under its own privacy policy.

Apple services

Game Center is optional. If you use it, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center under your Game Center profile. Sign in with Apple is optional too; you can use every feature as a guest. Both are covered by Apple's privacy policy. Reminders are scheduled on the device; no notification passes through a server.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs.
  • It does not sell anything.

Deleting your data

Delete Account in Settings removes your profile and everything stored under it. Deleting the app removes the rest.

If you have iCloud Backup switched on, your device backup may include Geografy's local data. That backup is covered by Apple's privacy policy, not this one.

Children

Geografy collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Geografy ever collects data, this page and the app's privacy manifest will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
