Jocker for iOS

Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 September 2026

Jocker collects nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you enter stays on your device, and the app never makes a network request.

What the app stores

Jocker keeps the following on your device only, using Apple's SwiftData framework:

  • Player names you type when starting a game.
  • The bids, tricks and trump you record for each hand.
  • Your settings — appearance, language, and the default rules for new games.

None of this is transmitted anywhere. It is not sent to the developer, and it is not shared with anyone else.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect or transmit personal data.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It contains no advertising and no third-party SDKs.
  • It does not require or offer an account.
  • It makes no network requests, and works with no connection at all.

Deleting your data

Deleting a game inside the app removes it from your device. Deleting the app removes everything Jocker has stored.

If you have iCloud Backup switched on, your device backup may include Jocker's local data. That backup is covered by Apple's privacy policy, not this one.

Children

Jocker is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Jocker ever collects data, this page and the app's privacy manifest will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
